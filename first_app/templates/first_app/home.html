Frequently asked before you order

Quick answers to the things we’re asked the most about fabrics, sizing and shipping.

How does your sizing run?

Most pieces are slightly relaxed. If you are between sizes, we suggest going with the smaller size for a neater fit or up for an oversized look.

Where do you ship?

We currently ship across Europe, the UK and North America, with 48h delivery available in major cities.

Can I return if it doesn’t fit?

Yes – unworn items with tags can be returned or exchanged within 30 days, no questions asked.
