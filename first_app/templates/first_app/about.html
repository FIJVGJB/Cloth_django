About us

The studio behind the clothes.

StyleHaus is an independent clothing label built by a small team of designers, photographers and product people who care about how everyday clothes feel, not just how they look.

We started in a one–room studio in 2020, frustrated with fast fashion and graphic-heavy basics that fell apart after a few washes. We wanted wardrobe staples that could survive coffee spills, airport security trays and late–night edits without losing their shape.

Every piece we make is tested in the real world – worn to shoots, packed into carry–ons and thrown into shared laundry machines – before it ever hits the online store.

What we care about
